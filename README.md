# Iran Mining AI Platform

A next-generation Mining Operations Intelligence Platform for Iranian mining companies.

## Core Modules

- Fleet Management System (FMS)
- Dispatch Optimization
- Predictive Maintenance
- Fuel Optimization
- Production Forecasting
- Mining Copilot (Persian AI Assistant)

## Target Customers

- National Iranian Copper Industries Company
- Golgohar
- Chadormalu
- Goharzamin
- IMIDRO

## Competitive Position

Designed as an AI layer on top of:
- MineStar
- Wenco
- Hexagon
- Existing local FMS solutions

## Business Goal

Increase mine productivity while reducing operational costs through AI-driven decision support.
